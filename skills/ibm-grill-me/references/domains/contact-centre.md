# Contact Centre Transformation

Domain-specific discovery for contact centre modernization.

## Channel Strategy
- What channels today? (voice, chat, email, SMS, social)
- Volume breakdown by channel?
- Which growing/declining?
- Cost per contact by channel?

## Technology Stack

### Telephony
- Platform? (Avaya, Cisco, Genesys, Five9, Amazon Connect)
- Version? On-prem or cloud?
- IVR system? Call flow complexity?
- SIP or TDM?

### CRM & Desktop
- What CRM? (Salesforce, Dynamics, ServiceNow)
- Integration with telephony?
- Agent desktop? How many screens?
- Wrap-up process?

### WFM & QA
- WFM tool? (Verint, NICE, Calabrio)
- Forecasting accuracy?
- QA tool? Recording coverage?
- Speech analytics?

### Quality

- Compliance failures?

### Cost

- Offshore vs onshore mix?
