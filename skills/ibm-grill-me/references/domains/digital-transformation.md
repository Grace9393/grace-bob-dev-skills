# Digital Transformation

Domain-specific discovery for enterprise transformation.

## Vision
- What does "digital transformation" mean here?
- What business outcomes?
- What's the burning platform?
- What happens if you don't transform?
- Vision 3-5 years out?

## Scope
- Enterprise-wide or specific BUs?
- What processes in scope?
- What systems being transformed?
- What's NOT changing?

## Current Maturity

### Process
- % manual vs automated?
- Where do paper forms exist?
- Most handoffs where?
- Data entered multiple times where?

### Technology
- Cloud adoption?
- API-first architecture?
- Mobile-first?
- Data architecture (siloed vs integrated)?
- DevOps maturity?

### Customer Experience
- Current journey?
- Omnichannel?
- Self-service capabilities?
- Personalization level?

### Data
- Data warehouse/lake?
- Real-time analytics?
- ML capabilities?
- Data governance?

## Transformation Pillars
- Customer experience focus?
- Operational excellence?
- Data & insights?
- Culture & ways of working?

## Stakeholder Alignment
- Who's the sponsor?
- C-suite aligned?
- Which BUs enthusiastic? Resistant?
- Pilots identified?

## Success Metrics
- Revenue target?
- Cost reduction target?
- CSAT improvement?
- Time-to-market improvement?
- Digital adoption %?

## Pitfalls
- Vague vision
- Technology-first approach
- Underestimating change mgmt
- Pilot purgatory
- Siloed transformation
- Ignoring technical debt

## Red Flags
- "We need to be like [tech company]"
- "Let's do everything at once"
- "IT will handle it"
- "We'll figure out ROI later"
- "Change management can wait"
