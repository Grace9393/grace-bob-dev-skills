# Salesforce Implementation

Domain-specific discovery for Salesforce CRM.

## Business Context
- Current CRM?
- What's broken?
- Why Salesforce? Why now?
- Which clouds? (Sales, Service, Marketing)
- Which edition? License count?

## Sales Process
- Lead-to-cash flow?
- Sales cycle length?
- Conversion rate at each stage?
- Territory model?
- Account hierarchies?
- Forecasting methodology?

## Data Migration
- Source systems?
- Data volume?
- How clean is data?
- Historical requirements?
- Deduplication strategy?
- Data mapping done?

## Integrations
- ERP? (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite)
- Marketing automation?
- E-commerce?
- Billing system?
- Real-time or batch?
- Middleware? (Mule, Boomi)

## Customization
- Standard objects sufficient?
- Custom fields needed?
- Validation rules? Workflows?
- How much custom code?
- Lightning or Classic?
- Mobile requirements?

## Security
- How many roles?
- Sharing model?
- Permission sets?
- Partner portal? Customer community?
- SSO? MFA?

## Reporting
- Standard reports OK?
- Dashboards for whom?
- Einstein Analytics?
- Third-party BI?

## Change Management
- How many users?
- Current tech-savviness?
- Training approach?
- How measure adoption?
- Change champions?

## Deployment
- Sandbox strategy?
- Release management approach?
- DevOps? CI/CD?
- Big bang or phased?
- Rollback plan?

## Governance
- Who will be admin?
- Support model post-launch?
- Enhancement request process?

## Red Flags
- "We'll figure out integrations later"
- "Users will just learn it"
- "Data is pretty clean"
- "We don't need customization"
- "IT will handle it"
