# Discovery Question Bank

200+ targeted questions organized by interrogation lens.

---

## 1. Objective & Outcomes

### Problem Definition
- What problem are we really solving?
- Who is experiencing this problem? How often?
- What's the impact if we don't solve it?
- What triggered this initiative now vs 6 months ago?
- What problem are we NOT solving?

### Success Criteria
- What does success look like 6/12 months from now?
- How will we measure success? What's the baseline?
- What leading/lagging

⏺ indicators?
- What would cause us to declare this a failure?

Business Value

- What's the quantified business value?
- How does this align with strategic priorities?
- What's the opportunity cost?
- Who gets promoted if this succeeds? Fired if it fails?

---
2. Current State

Process

- Walk through the current process end-to-end
- How long does each step take? Bottlenecks?
- What breaks today? How often? Workaround?
- Who touches this? What are their pain points?

Technology

- What systems are involved? (names, versions)
- How do they integrate?
- What data flows between them?
- Where is data mastered?
- Technical debt level?

Baseline Metrics

- Current metrics? (don't accept "slow" - how slow?)
- Volume per day/hour?
- Error rate? Downtime?
- Cost today?
- User satisfaction score?

---
3. Scope & Boundaries

Inclusion

- What's explicitly in scope?
- What features are must-haves?
- What geographies/units included?
- What data is in scope?

Exclusion

- What's explicitly out of scope?
- What are we NOT changing?
- What's nice-to-have but not essential?

Interfaces

- What other systems/teams does this touch?
- Who are upstream dependencies? Downstream consumers?
- What external parties involved?

Phasing

- What's phase 1 vs phase 2?
- What's MVP vs future?
- Where are logical cutpoints?

---
4. Stakeholders & Decisions

Identification

- Who are ALL stakeholders?
- Who uses this? Who's affected?
- Who has veto power? Who approves?
- Who's going to resist? Why?

Decision Rights

- Who makes final go/no-go?
- Who approves budget? Scope changes?
- Who signs off on technical decisions?
- Who declares success?

Concerns & Motivations

- What does each stakeholder care about?
- What's their success metric?
- What keeps them up at night?
- What's in it for them?

---
5. Constraints

Budget

- Total budget? (CapEx vs OpEx)
- What's included?
- Approval threshold?
- What happens if we go over?

Timeline

- What's the deadline? Flexible or hard?
- Why that date?
- What happens if we miss it?
- What's the critical path?

Compliance

- What regulations apply?
- What audit requirements?
- What security standards?
- Who validates compliance?

Technical

- What technologies are mandated/prohibited?
- What platforms must we support?
- Performance requirements?
- Scalability requirements?

---
6. Risks & Failure Modes

Technical Risks

- What could break? Blast radius?
- Single point of failure?
- What dependencies are brittle?
- Performance assumptions that might be wrong?

Schedule Risks

- What could delay us?
- What's on critical path?
- Where do we have no slack?
- What external dependencies could slip?

Adoption Risks

- What if users reject it?
- What if change management fails?
- What if training is insufficient?
- What if we lose productivity during transition?

---
7. Operating Model

Ownership

- Who owns this in production?
- Who's on-call? Escalation path?
- Who handles break-fix vs enhancements?
- Who funds ongoing operations?

Support

- What's the support model?
- Who provides user support?
- What's the runbook?

Governance

- How are changes approved?
- What's the change control process?
- Who's the long-term product owner?

---
8. Delivery Path

Approach

- Waterfall vs Agile vs hybrid?
- Build vs buy vs partner?
- Phased vs big bang?
- Pilot first?

Sequencing

- What must happen first?
- What can run in parallel?
- Where are decision gates?
- What's the MVP?

Quick Wins

- What can we deliver in 30 days?
- What low-hanging fruit?
- What proves the concept early?

---
Cross-Cutting Questions

Forcing Specificity

- "Can you give me an example?"
- "What do you mean by [vague term]?"
- "When you say 'soon', how soon?"
- "How expensive? $10K, $100K, $1M?"
- "How often is 'sometimes'?"

Uncovering Contradictions

- "Earlier you said X, now you're saying Y. Help me understand?"
- "If we can't have more budget AND can't extend timeline, what gives?"
- "You want real-time but source is batch. How do we resolve?"

Challenging Assumptions

- "What if we removed that requirement?"
- "Why do we assume that must be true?"
- "What evidence do we have?"
- "What would it cost to validate that?"

Exploring Edge Cases

- "What happens if we get 10x volume?"
- "What if the vendor goes out of business?"
- "What if users don't adopt it?"
- "What's the worst-case scenario?"

Testing Understanding

- "Let me summarize: [summary]. Accurate?"
- "So success means [X]. Do I have that right?"
- "Walk me through an example."
- "What am I missing?"
